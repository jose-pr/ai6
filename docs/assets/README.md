# Brand assets

The images here are AI6 project brand assets. They are raster-generated artwork, not
traced or vector sources; the icons include their own dark rounded-square backgrounds and
have no transparency.

| File | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai6-header.png` | 1294 × 278 | Banner at the top of the repository README |
| `outer-office-header.png` | 1294 × 278 | Banner at the top of the Outer Office README |
| `ai6-128.png` | 128 × 128 | AI6 icon, the documentation site's logo |
| `favicon.png` | 64 × 64 | AI6 icon, the documentation site's favicon |

Outer Office's icons are made from the Outer Office icon:
`clients/outer-office/web/favicon.png` (64 × 64), `clients/outer-office/web/icons/`
(192 and 512, plus maskable versions with the art at 80% on its edge colour) and
`clients/outer-office/windows/runner/resources/app_icon.ico` (16 to 256). Moneypenny,
the assistant inside it, keeps her own icon as her chat avatar:
`clients/outer-office/assets/brand/moneypenny-128.png` (128 × 128). The app's menu
heading uses the Outer Office icon at 256 px (`assets/brand/outer-office-256.png`). Its
Overview hero art is cut from the two banners: the world map from the left of
`ai6-header.png` (`assets/heroes/map.jpg`, 940 × 278) and the skyline from the right of
`outer-office-header.png` (`assets/heroes/skyline.jpg`, 774 × 278).
