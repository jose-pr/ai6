# Brand marks

Hand-drawn SVG masters, drawn 2026-10-30 as plain geometry (paths, circles, ellipses and
stroked lines; no fonts, no raster tracing, no stock or reference art). Each is a 1024 px
square with padding, with a separately tuned `small` drawing for 16 to 64 px.

| Mark | Files | Concept it derives from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer Office | `outer-office-{master,small}-*.svg` | Two slanted O letters in a round bore with long, uneven rifling (six blades in the master, four in the small drawing) |
| AI6 | `ai6-{master,small}-*.svg` | Flat shield badge with a serifed I so it never reads A6; the master adds a three-node coordination detail |
| AI6 seal | `ai6-seal-master-*.svg` | Ring seal for large presentation art only, no lettering, never an icon |
| Universal Exports | `universal-exports-{master,small}-*.svg` | Globe; the master adds a two-line sans wordmark drawn as strokes, the small drawing is the globe alone |
| Moneypenny | `moneypenny-{master,small}-*.svg` | Simplified profile with one hair cut; the small drawing has a larger nose and cut |

Variants: `dark` (for dark backgrounds: ivory and gold, Moneypenny in warm gold), `light`
(navy, Moneypenny burgundy) and `mono` (one colour, `#000000`; recolour by editing the one
fill). Backgrounds are transparent; the same geometry is used in every variant.

Rules: no numeral 7 anywhere, no gun-shaped controls, no MI6 crest or credential look.

Use the `small` drawing up to 48 px (Universal Exports up to 64 px) and the master above that.
`outer-office-256.png` and `moneypenny-128.png` remain the app's rasters until these marks
are reviewed; the platform icons are not yet generated from them.
